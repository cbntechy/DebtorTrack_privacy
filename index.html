Privacy Policy - DebtorTrack

Last updated: September 25, 2026

DebtorTrack is developed by LearnsToBuild. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. By using DebtorTrack, you agree to the terms described here.

DebtorTrack stores your debtor and invoice data only on your own device. We do not operate a server or database that collects or stores this information.

1. Information We Collect

DebtorTrack is designed to help small business owners track money owed to them. The information you enter - such as:

  • Debtor names and email addresses
  • Amounts owed and due dates

- is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data, cannot view it, and do not transmit it to any server we operate.

2. Gmail Access for Sending Reminders

To send payment reminder emails on your behalf, DebtorTrack ues your Gmail account. Specifically:

  • DebtorTrack only sends email you choose to send, using your own Gmail account.
  • We do not read, store, or share the contents of your Gmail inbox.
  • We do not access emails other than those sent through the App's reminder feature.

    • 3. Data We Do Not Collect

    • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
    • We do not use your data for advertising.
    • We do not store your debtor list, and your invoice amounts on any server we control.

    4. Data Storage & Security

    Because all debtor and invoice data lives on your device, its security depends on your device's own security (passcode, encryption, etc). If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us, since we never had a copy.

    5. Your Control Over Your Data

    • You can edit or delete any debtor entry directly within the App at any time.
    • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

    6. Children's Privacy

    DebtorTrack is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

    7. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy policy as DebtorTrack evolves (for example, if we later introduce optional cloud sync or additional reminder channels). Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
